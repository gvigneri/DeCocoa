## Identity
Manual page 3 describes six cocoa pods forming the Flower of Life. Pages 4-7 contain approved background variants. Pages 23-24 govern clear space and prohibited treatments; their rendered pages were inspected on October 11, 2026.

Page 23: X is the short dimension of a pod, used for the standalone mark. Y is the long dimension of a pod, used for the badge, vertical, and horizontal lockups. In the horizontal diagram Y is approximately half the height of the flower mark. Reserve at least that measured space around the actual artwork, not the artboard. Do not present a generic margin as the rule.

Page 24: no rotation, stretching, squishing, recoloring, altered lettering, rearrangement, logo on pattern, or logo used as part of a headline/sentence. Keep header/footer logo artwork static. Guy's October 11 homepage revision explicitly permits the original standalone logo icon as a photo mask; see `brand-exceptions.md`.

## Palette and type
Rendered pages 17 and 19 reviewed. Colors: orange #E87933, chocolate #512316 by approved exception, cream #F7EFE6, sunrise #FFBE4A, leaf #327D4F, fresh #A0B65C, blush #F2C9C1, ripe #DC4B33. Actual artwork may differ slightly; preserve it and document material differences.

Headlines: Montserrat Bold or Light; body: Montserrat Regular or Light. Use local Google Fonts Montserrat 300/400/700 with its OFL and provenance. Susa Regular is optional and omitted pending a commercial webfont license. Avenir is not presumed available. No wireframe serif styling.

## Patterns and provisional content
Preserve supplied AI files and faithful SVG master exports. Guy's October 11 revision permits website background instances with slow row motion and individual highlights in approved palette colors. Preserve geometry, retain static/reduced-motion states, and treat as placed covers until repeat edges are validated. Keep the full identity logo on quiet solid backgrounds.

Products are supplied as original PNGs. People/process/experience imagery embedded in the wireframe is reference material; mark extractions as provisional in the handoff. Partner/certification marks and operational claims require current approval before production. No sustainability, food-safety, or booking claims should be embellished.
