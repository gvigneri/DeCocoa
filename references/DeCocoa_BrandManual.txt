The De Cocoa logo has 
multiple meanings: 
Our new brand mark represents 6 cocoa pods 
coming together to create the Flower of Life.
The Flower of Life is an ancient geometric symbol 
that represents creation, connection, and unity.
In numerology the number six represents balance, 
harmony and responsibility.
