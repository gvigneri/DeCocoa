# Bonfire structural reference

Inspected October 11, 2026 at https://fjujdwrk.elementor.cloud/ in the browser. Desktop screenshots saved alongside this note. The site was accessible; the brief's earlier timeout is resolved.

Observed: four navigation families with nested destinations, separate contact CTA, broad introductory headline, paired exploration/contact actions, large image/video area, moving client strip with a pause control, alternating explanatory sections and service groups, multi-column footer, contact block, and separate legal row.

Useful structural ideas for DeCocoa: visible navigation families, distinct discovery and contact routes, a strong introduction followed by evidence and human story, and footer links grouped by purpose.

DeCocoa adaptation: shorter hero copy, original food imagery, no autoplay media or logo marquee, no repeated partner logos, varied section compositions, and solid logo backgrounds. Bonfire colors, serif type, gradients, customer claims, imagery, and contact details are not DeCocoa assets and will not be reused. Bonfire is a structural reference only; no code or content has been copied.

Limit: this was a desktop visual/structural inspection, not a full interaction or accessibility audit of Bonfire.
