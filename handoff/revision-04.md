# Homepage revision requested October 11, 2026

This revision follows Guy's direct feedback on homepage-r3. The previous reviewed package is preserved as `DeCocoa-preview-r3.zip`. The original supplied AI, PDF and PNG sources remain unchanged.

## Requested design changes

- Primary buttons: Orange background, Chocolate lettering, outer 2px Cream border with 16px corner radius, inner 1px Cream border with 12px corner radius.
- Secondary buttons: transparent background, Chocolate lettering, equivalent Orange borders and radii. Remove arrows from CTAs.
- Main header: centered stacked logo and navigation, with Let's Talk at the upper right. A separate Orange horizontal header appears on downward scrolling and withdraws on upward scrolling.
- Maximum content width: 1500px.
- Replace the hero chocolate-bar composition with supplied dipped-pretzel imagery and a restrained falling entrance.
- Use source patterns as substantial section backgrounds, convert all six AI patterns to editable SVGs, and allow subtle pod-row movement and changing pod colors within the existing palette.
- Use the original standalone icon as a photographic mask. This and pattern color/motion are explicit user-approved exceptions recorded in `brand/brand-exceptions.md`.
- Remove prototype explanations, provisional captions and unfinished-page dialogs from the visitor interface. Keep provenance and production decisions in the handoff. Message delivery remains disconnected; there must be no false sent-message confirmation.

## Logo correction

The prior custom SVG export skipped PDF rectangle commands. One such filled rectangle is the underline below the e in the supplied horizontal logo: source rectangle x1852.346, y844.482, width197.661, height44.004. This was an export error, not a different approved lockup. The revised exporter preserves the original geometry, including this underline, and distinguishes filled rectangles from clipping paths.
