Our Craft

We are built with innovation at its core, our facility blends tradition and technology to craft chocolate with precision and care.

From sourcing to production, we uphold the highest standards of quality, sustainability, and continuous improvement.

Promise

We promise to craft every chocolate with care, creativity, and integrity.

We always meet the highest standards of quality and food safety so you can taste and trust every bite.

Focus

At DeCocoa Chocolate Crafters, our focus is to combine passionate people, innovative craft, and sustainable practices to create chocolate with purpose and care.

Sustainability

We are committed to making chocolate that’s better for people and the planet. From responsible sourcing to eco-friendly packaging, we craft our chocolate using 100% sustainable power harnessed from the mighty energy of The Niagara Falls, leaving a lighter footprint on our Planet Earth.

Food Safety

We are committed to producing safe, high-quality, and authentic products that meet or exceed customer expectations.

We ensure:

  • Full compliance with all food safety laws and regulations, including adherence to the highest level of SQF food safety quality standards
  • Clear communication with employees, partners, suppliers, customers, and regulators
  • Ongoing training to empower our team and strengthen our Food Safety Culture
  • Continuous improvement in our processes, products, and services
