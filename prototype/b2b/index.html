Crafting the Future

We know chocolate. Our work is guided by consumer and market insight. Our chocolate crafters bring hands-on experience. We develop and test integrated solutions. The result is chocolate people enjoy.

Driving Your Brand is our mission.

Contract manufacturing, co-packing and fulfillment for private label chocolate goods. We deliver cost-effective quality, innovation, and all natural ingredients to your personalized finished product; generating added value and widening your profit margins. Your Brand is our top priority and we ensure your success with unmatched quality products since 1971. Our focus and experience is in mass producing both small batch and high volume production of quality chocolate products created under the strictest industry standards. Vigneri Chocolate sets itself apart with innovative artisan chocolates for the everyday and holiday category. We have a full range of off the shelf items and the capability to customize the flavor and shape of any finished product to bring excitement to your Private Label needs.

Why Partner with DeCocoa

We do more than make chocolate. We control the process from idea to delivery. This means consistent quality. It means efficiency at scale. It means a partner that meets you where you sell.

What We Do

  • Product Development
  • Ingredient sourcing and validation
  • Format and tooling support
  • End-to-end chocolate production and packaging
  • Scalable solutions with consistent quality

How We Do It

  1. Align

    We define specifications, volumes, quality standards, and timelines.

  2. Prepare

    We review ingredients, allergens, formats, and tooling.

  3. Produce

    We manage the full process in our integrated facility. From blending and refining to molding and packaging.

  4. Deliver

    We plan production and support efficient, reliable delivery as you scale

Built for Growth

We source responsibly. We choose ingredients with care. We work across the value chain to reduce impact. Our supply strategies are dependable and flexible. We scale with you without compromising quality.
