Begin with your product idea.

Tell us about the chocolate you want to explore and the context for your project. Shape, texture, finish and intended use can all help begin the conversation.

Connect the idea with the craft.

Explore mixing, one shot depositing and hollow moulding on our craft and product pages. Tell us which process interests you and the questions you would like to discuss.

A useful project brief

Bring the shape, texture, finish and process questions that matter to your project. Tell us how you imagine the chocolate being used and which details you want to explore.
