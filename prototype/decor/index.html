Chocolate as Art

At DeCocoa, chocolate is craft. Each piece is handmade, shaped by tradition, refined by modern technique. Pure ingredients. Clean design. Honest flavor. A sensory experience in every bite.

Décor

Gourmet decorating products catered to the Professional Chef. Artisanal chocolate décor innovations and customizable products serve food service channels; from bakeries to manufacturers.

Created by professionals, for professionals,

DeCocoa Decor™ offers innovative, customizable, and luxurious chocolate décor from precision ingredients to exquisitely finished decorative expressions. Rooted in cocoa and inspired by design, our creations reveal themselves in graceful silhouettes, layered color, and refined detail where collaboration awakens creativity and chocolate becomes an art of emotion.
