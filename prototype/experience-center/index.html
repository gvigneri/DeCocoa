How It’s Made

Follow the story of chocolate from cacao to the finished confection. The Experience Center offers a starting point for exploring the ingredients, stages and craft behind chocolate.

Discover the processes on our craft page, then ask about the chocolate-making experience that interests you.

Explore the idea of crafting your own chocolate bar and building a gift box. Let curiosity guide the shapes, finishes and chocolate ideas you would like to discuss.

Ask about Craft Your Own to find out more before planning a visit.

Museum & Virtual Exhibition

The Experience Center brings the history of chocolate and how it is made into the story. The museum and virtual exhibition invite a closer look at that journey.

The Green Screen Photo Room is another part of the experience described in our story. Ask about the experience you have in mind.

Tours and Classes

Factory tours, chocolate crafting classes and tasting sessions are ways to explore the craft described in the Experience Center story.

Start with an inquiry about the activity that interests you. Ask about visit arrangements and details before making plans.
