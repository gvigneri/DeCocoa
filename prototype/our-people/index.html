Our Roots

Our story begins in the cobblestoned streets of a Sicilian patisserie, where the Vigneri family first fell in love with the art of confection.

In the scent of warm pastries and the flicker of old ovens, a legacy of sweetness began to rise. Tradition, dreams and a devotion to the craft are part of that story.

Our Principals

Our vision, mission and focus bring people, craft and care together. They describe the direction behind our chocolate and the experiences we want to create.

Vision

A chocolate brand shaped by craftsmanship, creativity and care, with responsible sourcing and a farmers-first future as aspirations.

Mission

To craft chocolate with care and create memorable chocolate experiences.

Focus

To bring passionate people, innovative craft and sustainable practices together in chocolate with purpose.

Our Values

Integrity. Ingredients. Innovation. Invest. Inspire. These are the values named in our story, and the ingredients we bring to our work.
