OUR ROOTS

Our story begins in the cobble stoned streets of a Sicilian patisserie, where the Vigneri family first fell in love with the art of confection.

In the scent of warm pastries and the flicker of old ovens, a legacy of sweetness began to rise.

With hands guided by tradition and hearts full of dreams, the Vigneri name became known for its devotion to the craft.

Our Principals

VISION

Our vision is to be an industry leading chocolate brand known for craftsmanship, creativity, and care while building a farmers-first, sustainable future through ethical sourcing and responsible practices.

FOCUS

Our focus is to combine passionate people, innovative craft, and sustainable practices to create chocolate with purpose and care.

MISSION

We passionately handcraft the highest-quality chocolates and deliver the sweetest, most memorable experiences to our customers.

Our Values

Our values are the key ingredients in our chocolate and in everything we do:

Integrity

We stand by our promise of quality, care, and food safety in every step.

Inspire

We bring passion to our craft and encourage fresh ideas every day.

Ingredients

We choose the purest, most sustainable ingredients to create with purpose.

Innovation

We think ahead and push boundaries to craft the future.

Invest

We grow by investing in our people, our partners, and our community.
