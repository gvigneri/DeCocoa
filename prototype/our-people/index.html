Our Chocolate Crafters

We are committed to investing in our people and strengthening our community.

Rooted in Rochester, NY with a global perspective, we support local initiatives, foster a culture of inclusion, and create meaningful impact where we live and work.

Our Roots

Our story begins in the cobblestoned streets of a Sicilian patisserie, where the Vigneri family first fell in love with the art of confection. In the scent of warm pastries and the flicker of old ovens, a legacy of sweetness began to rise. With hands guided by tradition and hearts full of dreams, the Vigneri name became known for its devotion to the craft.

The Journey to America

Drawn by possibility and passion, The Vigneri Family brought their treasured recipes across the sea, planting roots in Rochester, New York. There, the legacy flourished blending Old World elegance with American spirit.

The Birth of DeCocoa

DeCocoa was born from Vigneri Chocolate as a tribute to the cocoa bean, the soul of chocolate, and to generations of craft and care. From bean to bite, we honor that legacy by creating chocolate that stirs memory, emotion, and delight.

Today

DeCocoa Chocolate Crafters is on a mission to passionately handcraft the highest-quality chocolates and deliver the sweetest, most memorable experiences to our customers.

Our vision is to be an industry-leading chocolate brand known for craftsmanship, creativity, and care while building a farmers-first, sustainable future through ethical sourcing and responsible practices. Our focus is to combine passionate people, innovative craft, and sustainable practices to create chocolate with purpose and care

  1. 1951- Nonno Gaetano Vigneri opened a pastry shop in Realmonte, Sicily.

  2. 1967- Filippo Vigneri moves to America and buys a European Bakery in Rochester, New York.

  3. 1971 – Vigneri Confection's brand is registered and Italian Specialties are sold wholesale nationwide.

  4. 2014 – Alex Vigneri restructures into Vigneri Chocolate, fulfilling three generations of confectioners.

  5. 2024- Alex Vigneri rebrands Vigneri Chocolate into DeCocoa Chocolate Crafters.

Our Values

Our values are the key ingredients in our chocolate and in everything we do:

Help each other. Learn. Grow as a team.

INSPIRE: LIFT OTHERS

Be positive. Be helpful. Set a good example.

Try new things. Solve problems. Find a better way.
